School System A1A2

The German school system varies slightly by state (Bundesland), but the main structure is consistent across Germany.

School levels — from youngest to oldest

Age 0–6
die Kita / der Kindergarten
Daycare / Preschool — not compulsory. Focus on play and development.
Age 6–10
Klasse 1–4
die Grundschule
Primary school — compulsory for all children. Teaches reading, writing, arithmetic.
Age 10–15/16
Klasse 5–9/10
die Hauptschule (→ Hauptschulabschluss)
Lower secondary school. Practical focus. Leads to vocational training (Ausbildung).
Age 10–16
Klasse 5–10
die Realschule (→ mittlere Reife)
Intermediate secondary school. Mix of academic and practical. Opens doors to many vocational and some academic paths.
Age 10–18/19
Klasse 5–12/13
das Gymnasium (→ das Abitur)
Academic secondary school. The most demanding track. Ends with Abitur (university entrance qualification).
Age 10–16
Klasse 5–10
die Gesamtschule
Comprehensive school — combines all three tracks. Students can achieve any qualification level.
Age 16+
die Berufsschule / die Ausbildung
Vocational school + apprenticeship. Dual system: learn at school + work at a company (3 years).
Age 18+
die Universität / die Hochschule
University. Requires Abitur. Bachelor → Master → Doktor.
Age 18+
die Fachhochschule (FH)
University of applied sciences. More practical focus. Requires Fachabitur or Abitur.

German grading system

GradeMeaningEquivalent
1 — sehr gutvery goodA
