Slang, Curses & Insults A2+

Achtung! German profanity is often more direct than English. Use these with extreme caution; they are provided here for recognition and to understand informal movies or street language.

Common Exclamations

Scheiße!Shit! (Very common)
Verdammte Scheiße!Goddamn shit!
Mist!Crap! / Dang! (Mild)
Verdammt!Dammit!
Fahr zur Hölle!Go to hell!
Das ist mir scheißegal.I don't give a shit.
Nicht mein Problem!Not my problem.

Direct Commands

Halt die Fresse!Shut the fuck up (Aggressive)
Halt den Mund / Schnauze!Shut up!
Verpiss dich!Piss off! / Fuck off!
Verschwinde aus meinem Gesicht!Get out of my face!
Leck mich am Arsch!Kiss my ass! (Literal: Lick)
Fick dich!Fuck you!
