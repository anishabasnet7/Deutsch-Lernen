Prefix A2

Separable prefixes split from the verb in main clauses: Ich mache die Tür auf. In infinitive form they stay together: aufmachen. In subordinate clauses they stay together: …weil er aufmacht.

On / Off — an / aus

an- = on, starting, approaching
anmachen → to turn ON (light, TV)
anschalten → to switch ON
ankommen → to arrive
anrufen → to call (phone)
anfangen / beginnen → to start/begin
anziehen → to put on (clothes) / attract
antworten → to answer
Ich mache das Licht an. I turn the light on.
aus- = off, out, finished
ausmachen → to turn OFF (light, TV)
ausschalten → to switch OFF
ausgehen → to go out
ausziehen → to take off (clothes) / move out
aussteigen → to get off (transport)
ausfüllen → to fill in (a form)
Mach das Licht aus! Turn the light off!

Open / Close — auf / zu

auf- = open, up, onto
aufmachen → to OPEN
aufstehen → to get UP / stand up
aufhören → to stop / cease
aufräumen → to tidy UP
aufschreiben → to write down
aufwachen → to wake UP
Ich mache das Fenster auf. I open the window.
zu- = close, toward, addition
zumachen → to CLOSE / shut
zuhören → to listen (attentively)
zunehmen → to gain weight / increase
zugehen → to walk toward
zurückkommen → to come back
Mach die Tür zu! Close the door!

In / Out — ein / aus

ein- = into / in
einsteigen → to get IN (transport)
einschlafen → to fall asleep
einladen → to invite
einkaufen → to shop
einchecken → to check in
einziehen → to move IN (to a home)
Ich steige in den Bus ein. I get on the bus.
aus- = out / exit
aussteigen → to get OUT (transport)
ausziehen → to move OUT (of a home)
ausgehen → to go out
Ich steige aus dem Zug aus. I get off the train.

Come / Go direction — her / hin

her- = movement TOWARD the speaker (come here)  |  hin- = movement AWAY from speaker (go there)
her- = toward me/us (come)
herkommen → to come here
herbringen → to bring here
hereinkommen → to come in
rüber / herüber → over here
Komm her! Come here!
hin- = away from me (go there)
hingehen → to go there
hinlegen → to lay down
hinausgehen → to go out
rüber / hinüber → over there
Geh hin! Go there!

More key prefixes

mit- = together/along
mitkommen → come along
mitnehmen → take along
mitmachen → participate
ab- = off/away/down
abfahren → to depart
abholen → to pick up
abnehmen → lose weight / remove
abschreiben → to copy
vor- = before/forward
vorstellen → to introduce
vorbereiten → to prepare
vorbeikommen → to pass by
vorlesen → to read aloud
nach- = after/following
nachkommen → to follow after
nachfragen → to ask again
nachdenken → to think about
zurück- = back
zurückkommen → to come back
zurückgehen → to go back
zurückgeben → to give back
los- = starting/releasing
losgehen → to start / set off
losfahren → to drive off
loslassen → to let go
fort-/weg- = away
fortgehen → to go away
weggehen → to go away
wegfahren → to drive away
um- = around/change
umsteigen → to change trains
umziehen → to move/change clothes
umsehen → to look around
rüber-/herüber- = across/over here
rüberkommen → come over
herüberschicken → send over
Komm rüber! → Come over!
