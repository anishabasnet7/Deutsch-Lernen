sp / st — at the START of a word or syllable

At the START of a word/syllable: sp = "shp" and st = "sht"
sp- (start)"shp"spielen, sprechen, Sport, Sprache, Spaß → shpeelen, shprekhen
st- (start)"sht"Staat, Straße, stehen, Student, Stunde → shtaat, shtraase
In the MIDDLE or END of a word: sp and st are pronounced normally as "sp" and "st".
